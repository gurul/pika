Saved front: —° raw. Aim the sonar straight down the car's axis, at a wall or a box, and nudge until it reads the shortest distance. Hold an arrow to keep turning, or use Shift+←→. Saving stores this angle as 90° in calibration.json, for every tool.

Autonomous

Swivel sweeps the sonar 10–170° and back, and keeps sweeping while you drive. Any other drive input returns the car to manual. Moving the pan slider ends the swivel.
Follow: press it, then click the person in the video. The car keeps about 30 cm behind them and steers to keep them centred. It stops when it loses them for more than a moment, when this page closes, and on any drive key. Click someone else in the video to switch.
